# Third-party assets

## Muscle map illustrations (`MuscleFront`, `MuscleBack` in Assets.xcassets)

Two plates from Julien Bouglé, *Le corps humain en grandeur naturelle : planches coloriées et
superposées, avec texte explicatif* (Paris: J. B. Baillière et fils, 1899), digitised by the U.S.
National Library of Medicine (Historical Anatomies on the Web, NLM Unique ID 0227562) and hosted
on Wikimedia Commons.

- Front (anterior) view: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bougle_whole2_retouched.png
  (retouched by Wikipedia user The Anome, who placed the retouching in the public domain).
- Back (posterior) view: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bougle_whole5.jpg

Author: Julien Bouglé (died 1900). Licence: public domain (published 1899; the author's life plus
70 years has expired; US public domain as published before 1931; Wikimedia Commons PD-Art / PD-old).
No attribution is required; this note records the source.

Modifications made for the app (also public domain): background removed, colours muted by
30 percent, the groin on the front plate painted over, downscaled to 1500 px tall.
